Choose your role

Partner and admin need that role's key — the hub prints both when it starts, or you set them with GLM_KEYS. User needs no key: lending your own address grants no access to anyone else's.

◇
User

Lends a little.

  • Offer your IP as an egress point
  • Solve captchas for the pool
  • Keep a tab open, stop any time
  • Cannot add tokens or sessions
◆
Partner

Lends accounts.

  • Add tokens and sessions
  • Unlock more of the account pool
  • Solve captchas for the pool
  • Offer your IP as egress
◈
Admin

Runs the pool.

  • Everything user and partner do
  • See partners, tokens and egress
  • Change per-model features
  • Clear sessions, drop partners
✦
Superadmin

Owns the directory.

  • Everything admin does
  • Seeded at startup, one account
  • The only role that may manage people
  • Needs its own key, like admin

The key is only sent to this hub to get a session. It is never stored in this page, and the hub never echoes it back.

All three roles mint captchas — that is the point of the pool. The differences are what each one may contribute and what each one may see.

Sign-in options, honestly

  • A keyed session, no account. The hub checks the role's key in constant time, then issues a signed HttpOnly cookie. The role is granted by the key, not by you picking it — the admin key is the rule for who may claim admin.
  • Where the keys come from: the hub generates a random one per privileged role at startup and prints it, or you pin them with GLM_KEYS. Keys are per-role, not per-person — except the superadmin, which is seeded as one named account (GLM_SUPERADMIN).
  • Every page needs a session. Opening one while signed out redirects here; only this page and its styling stay public.
  • What is still missing: key rotation and revocation, and persistence — a restart rotates the generated keys and drops every session, though a superadmin pinned with GLM_SUPERADMIN survives.
  • Why it matters: the mint socket is refused without a session, and /status needs the admin role — so a stranger who reaches the port gets 401s, not the partner list or free mints.
