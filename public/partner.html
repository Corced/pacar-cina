Add a token or a session

sign in first — the hub needs a session to accept a donation
Read this before pasting anything real. With a hub listening and you signed in, submitting sends the value to the hub over HTTPS/localhost, which keeps only a fingerprint (a short SHA-256 hash) of it — never the value itself, in its log or anywhere else — plus your person name if you signed in with one. The hub then checks it straight away against Aliyun's captcha API and records what came back: valid, invalid, or unverified when it could not reach Aliyun at all. So the starting status is "verifying", and it settles within seconds — an unverified donation means "never proven", not "proven good". Nothing spends a held token yet. Do not paste a credential you could not afford to lose.
