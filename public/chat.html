What this page does, and what it does not

  • Sends each turn to POST /v1/chat/completions on this origin and shows the reply, the conversation id and the hub's own headers.
  • Sends the full history it is showing, so the hub never has to guess what was said. It detects the replay by the opening message, so nothing is duplicated.
  • Stores nothing: no message text in local storage, no cookie of its own. Reload and the transcript is gone; the hub's copy of the conversation survives.
  • Streams nothing and sends no tools or images — stream:false, plain text in, plain text out.
  • Proves nothing about a model's features. A suffix such as -think is a feature the catalog declares; whether the far end honours it is not exercised here.

The hub keeps at most 6000 characters of a conversation and drops the oldest turns first. A restart forgets every id, and the oldest is evicted once 200 exist — then an old id answers 404 and this page offers a fresh start instead of quietly dropping it.
